Identity & MoodTone

Calm, tactile, blueprint-style SaaS aesthetic — a printed spec sheet resting on a sage-green plaster desk.

01
Color TokensExact hex

Sage #869A8C + grain & vignette → sheet #F8F8F8 · ink #171717 · mint #5CB8A6 accents · one dark card #1F1F1F.

02
TypographyMono labels

Mono uppercase micro-labels (JetBrains Mono, +0.08em) · grotesque sans body (Inter 13–14px) · sans breadcrumb nav.

03
Spacing & RadiusScales

Spacing steps 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · radius hierarchy sheet 24 → card 14 → row 10 → pill 999.

04
Layout SystemSheet flow

One centered 760–880px sheet · flat white cards with dotted connectors · nested rows, chips and 4-column grids.

05
Depth & MotionSubtle

Flat hairline cards · fade + 4px slide 150–200ms ease-out · hover lifts 1px · soft shadows on sheet & dark card only.

Do & Don't — 10 rules
01

DO · Grain placement

grain only on the green background — never on white cards

02

DO · Mono labels

every label mono uppercase with +0.08em tracking

03

DO · Semantic mint

green only as small semantic accents, never large surfaces

04

DO · Generous air

at least 24px vertical gap between cards

05

DO · Pill everything

chips & status pills fully rounded with hairline borders

06

DON'T · Saturated fills

no saturated colors or large colored surfaces

07

DON'T · Shadow spam

no shadow on every element — sheet & dark card only

08

DON'T · Sharp corners

no 0px corners anywhere

09

DON'T · Gradient cards

no glassmorphism, no gradients on cards

10

DON'T · Dark card spam

max ONE dark terminal card per screen
